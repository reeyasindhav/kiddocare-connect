A platform where parents find trusted babysitters and childcare providers with verified profiles and reviews.

design ideas: warm friendly pallette, caregiiver profile cards with trust badges, availability calender, background check indicators, parent review system.

Problem Statement:

Working parents and guardians often face significant anxiety, friction, and safety concerns when searching for reliable short-term childcare. The traditional process relies heavily on informal word-of-mouth recommendations or unvetted local listings, which lack transparent background verification, standardized safety badges and authenticated parent reviews. Additionally, coordinating schedules manually leads to communication delays and scheduling conflicts. 

Kiddocare addresses this challenge by providing a centralised, transparent platform that connects parents with thoroughly vetted, trustworthy babysitters and childcare providers through real-time availability tracking, verified profiles, and authentic community feedback.
